Safe Trading Checklist

  • Check In-Game Stats: Steam trade windows do not display hidden stat boosts. Confirm weapon attributes in-game before trading.
  • Beware 7-Day Hold: Remember that any skin bought from the Steam Market cannot be traded for 7 days.
  • Avoid Off-Platform Payments: Never trade Steam items for external real-money transfers or third-party codes to avoid trade scams.
