Welcome to the Safehouse

PAYDAY 2 is an action-packed, four-player co-op shooter that lets gamers don the masks of the original PAYDAY crew—Dallas, Hoxton, Wolf, and Chains—as they descend on Washington D.C. for an epic crime spree. This fan page serves as a quick reference guide for character profiles, build meta ideas, cosmetics, trading tips, and strategy guides.

The Original Crew (Click a heister for details)

Mastermind

Dallas

The de facto leader of the Payday Gang. Known for his iconic American Flag mask and his urgent need for medic bags.

Fugitive

Hoxton

The sharp-tongued Brit who reclaimed his title after breaking out of custody in one of the gang's most memorable heists.

Technician

Wolf

A former software developer turned unpredictable wild card. Wolf handles heavy machinery, explosives, and drill repairs.

Enforcer

Chains

A military veteran who brings massive firepower and heavy armor support to every high-stakes job.

Popular Build Archetypes

Anarchist Armor-Gate

Focuses on rapid armor regeneration on hit, letting you continuously absorb damage while dealing high DPS.

  • Perk Deck: Anarchist
  • Armor: Lightweight Ballistic Vest / Suit
  • Key Skill: Bullseye (Basic)

Hacker Dodge

Uses Pocket ECMs to stun enemies and restore health to the entire team while maintaining high dodge chances.

  • Perk Deck: Hacker
  • Armor: Two-Piece Suit
  • Key Skill: Sneaky Bastard

Kingpin Tank

Uses the Injector to convert incoming damage into health, allowing you to survive open fire during crucial objective pushes.

  • Perk Deck: Kingpin
  • Armor: Improved Combined Tactical Vest
  • Key Skill: Iron Man (Ace)
