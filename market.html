Skins are traded for skins, never for money. Each listing shows what the seller wants in return, measured in Steam Community Market value. To buy, send the seller a Steam trade offer with skins worth about that much. Never send money or gift codes outside Steam. PD2Hub does not hold items, so check the seller's profile and read every item in a trade offer carefully before you accept it. Every listing is checked against the seller’s public Steam inventory the moment it’s created — look for the ✓ Verified tag. Item name, condition, rarity and picture come straight from the seller’s Steam inventory — sellers only set the asking value.
