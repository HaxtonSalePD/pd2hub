House Rules

What We Store, How the Giveaway Works, and How to Trade Safe

Home Trading Market Giveaway

The Short Version

  • PD2Hub is a free fan project run by one person. No ads, no tracking, no money.
  • We store the minimum Steam gives us: your public profile and your trade URL. Never your password, never your items.
  • The giveaway is free to enter, one entry per account, winners drawn at random.
  • On the market, skins trade for skins through Steam. Anyone asking you for money or codes is a scammer — report them.
  • Want your data gone? Ask, and it's deleted.

What We Store About You

Nothing is collected until you sign in through Steam or submit something. Visitors who only read the site are not tracked at all.

What we never see or store

  • Your Steam password. Signing in happens entirely on Steam's own page. Steam only tells us who you are.
  • Your items. We read your public inventory to verify listings, but the site can never move, hold or trade anything. Every trade is a normal Steam trade offer between two players.
  • Payment details. There is nothing to buy here.

In your browser

The site keeps a few things in your own browser's storage: your login token (valid 7 days), your theme choice, and small conveniences like which market tab you had open. This stays on your device. There are no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and item pictures load from Steam's servers — standard for any site showing Steam items.

Logging out (the ✕ next to your name) removes the login from your browser.

Getting your data removed

Message the site admin on Steam (Haxton Sale) and everything stored about your account — entries, listings, trade URL, the lot — gets deleted. No questions, no waiting period beyond the time it takes to read the message.

Giveaway Rules

  1. Entering is always free. Sign in with Steam, drop your trade URL, done. Nobody will ever ask you to pay, deposit, or "verify" anything to enter or to claim a prize.
  2. One entry per Steam account per giveaway. Duplicate accounts get removed.
  3. Winners are drawn at random — every entry has exactly the same chance, and the site's code enforces it.
  4. Prizes arrive as a normal Steam trade offer to the trade URL you entered with. Check that the offer asks for nothing from your side before accepting.
  5. Unclaimed prizes get redrawn. If a winner doesn't accept the trade offer within 14 days, a new winner is drawn.
  6. A giveaway can be cancelled before a draw; entries carry no cost either way.
  7. This giveaway is run by a fan. It is not sponsored by or affiliated with Starbreeze, OVERKILL or Valve.

Market Rules

  • Skins trade for skins — never for money. The dollar figure on a listing is a value guide, not a price tag. Never send real money, gift cards or codes.
  • Listings are checked against the seller's inventory when they're created and re-checked afterwards — if an item leaves the seller's inventory, its listing shrinks or disappears. Name, condition, rarity and picture come from Steam and can't be edited.
  • PD2Hub never holds items. Trades happen directly between players through Steam trade offers. Read every trade offer carefully before accepting.
  • Listings expire after 30 days, and each player can have at most 20 at a time.
  • See something shady? Use the report button on the listing. Reported listings are reviewed and scams are removed.
  • The admin can remove any listing that breaks these rules.

The Fine Print

PD2Hub is a hobby project, provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. It can go down, change or shut off. Trade sensibly: the site verifies what it can, but the final check before you accept any trade offer is always yours.
